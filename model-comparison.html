Every model, and which one to pick

Four video renderers, three image models — all available on every Pheli AI generation, right from the model picker next to the prompt box. Here's what actually differs between them, so picking one isn't a guess.

Video models

OpenAI
Sora 2
Best for: ads with sound
  • Generates native audio alongside the video — dialogue, ambience, sound effects
  • Fixed durations: 4, 8, 12, 16, or 20 seconds per segment
  • sora-2-pro unlocks 1080p ("Cinematic" quality)
  • "Continue this clip" extends the story as a new same-length segment
Google
Veo 3
Best for: physical realism
  • Strongest at physically accurate motion — weight, momentum, fluid dynamics
  • Flexible durations, not locked to a fixed set like Sora
  • "Continue this clip" genuinely extends the same take, not a new one
  • 1080p available under Cinematic quality
Kuaishou
Kling
Best for: fast iteration
  • Two model tiers — 2.1 and 2.5 Turbo — pick speed vs. quality directly
  • Fixed 5s or 10s clips, "pro" mode for the higher-fidelity render tier
  • Strong at stylized and illustrative motion, not just photoreal
  • No clip continuation yet — each render is a standalone clip
ByteDance
Seedance
Best for: 4K and reference-driven scenes
  • The only renderer here with a real 4K tier ("4k-sr")
  • "Reference to video" mode — feed it up to 10 reference images and it weaves them into the scene, not just a single starting frame
  • Two model tiers — 2.0 and 2.5 — flexible 4–30s durations
  • Generates native audio alongside the video, like Sora

A simple way to decide (video)

If the ad needs a voiceover or sound design baked into the render itself, Sora 2 or Seedance are the only two here that generate audio natively. If the shot depends on something moving convincingly — water, cloth, a crowd — Veo 3 tends to hold up best under scrutiny. If you're iterating fast on a concept and want to try a lot of variations quickly, Kling's range of speed/quality tiers makes that cheap. If you need real 4K output or you're animating a specific character/product across multiple reference images, Seedance is the only one of the four that can do either.

Image models

All three cover the same style chips — Photoreal, Illustration, 3D render, Animation — the difference is which provider is actually rendering behind the scenes, and what that provider tends to be strongest at.

OpenAI
GPT Image 1
Best for: text and layout
  • Strongest of the three at rendering legible text inside the image itself
  • Reliable prompt adherence for specific object counts, layouts, and composition
  • Good general-purpose default across all four style chips
Google
Nano Banana Pro
Best for: highest quality
  • Gemini's higher-fidelity image tier (gemini-3-pro-image)
  • Worth the extra render time when the output is the final deliverable
Google
Nano Banana 2
Best for: fast iteration
  • Gemini's faster, lighter image tier (gemini-3.1-flash-image)
  • Cheapest way to try several prompt variations before committing to a final render

None of this is a permanent choice — every template and every generation lets you swap models per render, so trying the same prompt on two models costs nothing but time.
