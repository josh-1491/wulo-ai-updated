Motion Control

Apply motion from a video to your subject

Upload a subject image and a motion reference clip — Seedance weaves them together so your subject performs that motion, instead of animating from a single starting frame.

This runs on Seedance 2.5's "reference to video" mode, which accepts a driving video alongside a subject image. It's the closest real, API-backed equivalent to Kling's consumer-app "Motion Control" feature — Kling's own developer API doesn't expose that feature directly, as far as could be confirmed, so this isn't literally the same model.
